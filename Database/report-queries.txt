Useful report ideas for viva and demonstration:

1. Total registered patients
- Count all documents in patients collection.

2. Total consultations done
- Count all documents in consultations collection.

3. Gender wise count
- Group patients by gender.

4. Age group report
- Group patients into Below 18, 18-40, 41-60, Above 60.

5. Disease trend report
- Group consultations by diagnosis and count frequency.

6. Search report
- Search by patient name or patient ID.

7. Filter report
- Filter consultations by doctor, date, or disease.
