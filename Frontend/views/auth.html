How It Works

  • Admin does not register. Use fixed login: admin@gmail.com / Admin@123.
  • Patient can self-register for camp and download own report.
  • Doctor registers with name, email, contact number, and specialization.
  • Registration stores data in MongoDB and redirects to the login page for that role.
